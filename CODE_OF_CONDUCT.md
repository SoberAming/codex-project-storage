# Community conduct / 社区行为规范

This project welcomes practical questions, reproducible reports, technical disagreement, and contributions in Chinese or English. Treat people with respect and discuss the work in terms others can assess.

## Expected behavior

- Explain the problem and evidence clearly; distinguish observations, assumptions, and untested claims.
- Be patient with different levels of technical experience and language fluency.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and credit others' work.
- Respect project scope, user authorization, privacy, and ownership of data and code.
- Keep reproductions fictional and disclose security-sensitive details through the private route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, unwanted personal contact, spam, malicious payloads, and intentionally misleading claims are not welcome. Do not publish another person's private files, contact details, credentials, or local reports without permission. Do not pressure contributors or users to run destructive tests against real data.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, redirect discussions, close or lock threads, restrict participation, or use GitHub's moderation tools. Actions should be proportionate to the behavior and protect affected people. Technical disagreement alone is not misconduct.

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact a maintainer through an explicitly offered channel or use GitHub's available reporting and moderation features. Do not disclose private details publicly to request moderation. No private contact address, confidential intake service, or response deadline is designated by this document.

These expectations apply to project-managed repositories and discussion spaces.
